How does ESL differ from a standard repair marketplace?

ESL is an integrated engineering firm established in 2008, with electrical, mechanical and telecommunications services for commercial, industrial and residential environments. Contact us to scope your specific project or site requirements.

Can ESL provide maintenance agreements for estates and offices?

Planned maintenance and service-level requirements can be discussed for your sites. Contact our team to confirm availability, geographic coverage, response times and contract terms for your needs.

Can you assess a solar or hybrid power transition?

Get in touch to discuss your facility's power profile and the options you are exploring. System design, equipment, financing and installation scope are confirmed with the ESL team.

Are ESL engineers COREN-certified?

Yes. ESL's leadership is COREN-registered, and our field technicians undertake safety and technical training aligned with Nigerian operating environments.

Where in Nigeria does ESL operate?

ESL serves sites across Nigeria, with teams operating in Lagos and major industrial hubs. Contact us to confirm coverage and deployment timelines for your location.
